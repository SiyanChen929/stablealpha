# Provenance and contribution

This public snapshot was assembled on 14 October 2026 from the owner's existing research files. `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` records hashes of distributed code. The paper is an October 2026 working manuscript; repository publication does not indicate journal acceptance.

The `cogalpha_plus` package comes from the earlier engineering prototype. It is distributed with its original numerical implementation and selected offline tests. No new factor search, model fit, or market evaluation was run to prepare this repository. Public synthetic-demo checks verify software behavior only.

The later controlled study used separately frozen implementations. Its experiment histories and data are outside this snapshot. The paper's result statements must be read with their exact protocols and reported scope.

The project's multi-view research architecture adapts CogAlpha. Its Alpha158 comparison uses Qlib's public baseline. Siyan Chen is responsible for the research direction, specifications, review, decisions, and interpretation; AI coding assistants supported substantial implementation and writing. The paper contains its AI-use statement and references.
