# Research overview

The research question is the marginal value of another proposal, another refinement, or a richer information dictionary. The measurement distinguishes a factor's individual training score, the accuracy of a model that combines factors, and the net return of the resulting holdings.

The paper combines historical search records, matched-library continuations, and discovery from an empty library. Controls compare search policies, proposal budgets, information sets, and source fields provided directly to the predictor. The learner and account rule are held fixed within each comparison. Rank correlation and portfolio returns are reported separately.

TRAIN covers 2010–2019 and development covers 2020–2022. Development observations were previously exposed. Separate later-period evaluations retain their own freeze and access history. Fresh seeds replicate algorithmic behavior on the stated data; they do not create an independent market sample.

The expanded financial dictionary retains current-vintage and availability-proxy limits. The findings concern this cohort, data construction, and fixed downstream pipeline. The manuscript is the authority for the exact account, proposal counts, comparisons, estimates, intervals, and tests.

The practical research lesson is to evaluate additional research effort on the downstream decision it is meant to improve, while measuring where gains disappear between formula, predictor, and holdings.
